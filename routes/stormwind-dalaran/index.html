Questions

Do I need a ship, a zeppelin or the tram?
Yes, on foot the way uses: Deeprun Tram from Stormwind City to Ironforge.
Can Horde players make this trip?
Yes, in about 28 min on foot. Alliance territory: guards and players will attack you.
Are these times exact?
They are estimates, give or take a quarter: flight times come from real Classic flights, walking times from the real size of each zone, and ships and zeppelins count up to five minutes of waiting.
